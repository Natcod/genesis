Step-by-Step Guidance

Admission & Visa Processing Roadmap

Genesis Consultancy ensures your application from Ethiopia to India is smooth and error-free.

01. Document Preparation
  • • Educational documents & academic transcripts authenticated
  • • Yellow fever vaccination certificate (from Tikur Anbessa Hospital)
  • • Valid original passport & copy
  • • 6-month Bank Statement confirming financial capacity
  • • 4 White background passport photos
02. Fees & Evaluation
  • • Application Fee: $150 USD
  • • Registration Fee (RF): $100 USD
  • • University reviews and tests eligibility of academic documents
  • • Issuance of Official Offer Letter (OL) upon acceptance
03. Visa & Final Acceptance
  • • Final Admission Letter (FAL) & Visa Letter sent to student
  • • Visa payment ($4,700 – $10,000 cash depending on program)
  • • Simple embassy visa application process assistance
  • • Note: Cash carried must strictly adhere to currency regulations
